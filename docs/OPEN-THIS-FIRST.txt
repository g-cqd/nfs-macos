NEED FOR SPEED (2015) - COMPLETE PORTABLE APP - OPEN THIS FIRST

Need for Speed Bundled 20261002-7d108a4-portable-complete.app

WHAT IT IS
A Mac app that runs Need for Speed (2015) through Wine. It carries your own licensed game files and EA's own
EA app, exactly as EA's installer installs it, so you do not download EA's installer or run an install step.
It carries NO EA account, no saved sign-in, no activation record and no save: you sign in with your own EA
account, in EA's own window, and EA decides whether you may play.
It is for your personal use on your own Macs. Do not share, upload or redistribute it.

WHAT YOU NEED
- An Apple silicon Mac (M1 or later) with macOS 15 or later.
- Rosetta 2 (the app has an "Install Rosetta..." button; it needs the internet once).
- About 40 GB of free disk space while the app prepares itself the first time (the app is about 16 GB; its
  own Windows folder, the EA app and EA's downloads come on top; if the app is on another disk than your
  Library folder, the first start copies the game, which needs about 16 GB more).
- Your own EA account that owns Need for Speed (2015), and the internet for the sign-in and EA's one-time
  activation. The first start itself needs no internet.

STEP 1 - LET macOS OPEN THE APP (ONCE, ONLY IF macOS BLOCKS IT)
The app is signed by its developer but NOT notarized by Apple (the owner decided against it). When an app
reaches your Mac by AirDrop, a download, mail, a USB stick or an unzipped archive, macOS marks it as
"quarantined" and refuses to open an app Apple has not checked: "Apple could not verify ... is free of
malware". The mark is only a note that the file came from outside; it does not say the file is bad or good.
Open the app only if you trust where this copy came from. Then do ONE of these:

  A. Try to open the app once. Then open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll to "Security", and
     press "Open Anyway" next to this app's name. (On macOS 15 and later, Control-click > Open no longer
     works for an app that is not notarized.)

  B. Remove the mark yourself. Open Terminal, type this and a space, but do not press Return yet:
         xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine
     Drag the app from Finder into the Terminal window (it types the path for you) and press Return.
     That is the same as:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/path/to/the app.app"

A copy that never left this Mac, or that came on a drive that does not set the mark, opens without this step.
Moving the app to your Applications folder (or removing the mark) also makes the first start faster: while the
mark is on, macOS runs the app from a temporary read-only copy and the first start must copy all of the game.

STEP 2 - OPEN THE APP AND WAIT
The first start takes a few minutes, once. The app builds its own private Windows folder in
~/Library/Application Support/NFS2015Mac, installs the EA app that came with it, and copies and checks the
game files. A progress bar shows the game files. Nothing is sent anywhere.

STEP 3 - SIGN IN
Press "Open EA App". EA's own window opens; sign in with your own EA account, then quit the EA app.
This app never sees, stores or copies your sign-in.

STEP 4 - PLAY
Press "Play". Expect two prompts that do not come from this app:
- EA may ask you to activate the game on this Mac, once (EA sees a Mac it has not seen).
- macOS may ask whether the app may use the microphone (the game's voice chat). Answer as you prefer.

IF SOMETHING IS WRONG
- "Open Log" in the app shows what the helper did.
- "Reinstall EA App..." exists only for a newer EA app than the one that came with this app (it needs the
  installer from ea.com).
- To start over, quit the app and move ~/Library/Application Support/NFS2015Mac to the Trash. That removes the
  Windows folder, the EA app's state, your EA sign-in on this Mac and the game's settings and saves.
- Not supported: Intel Macs, macOS 14 or earlier, a Mac without Rosetta.

WHAT WAS AND WAS NOT TESTED
Tested on the Mac that built the app: the first start in a clean environment with the network switched off, the
app copied to a different folder, the EA app starting from the app's own copy and showing EA's sign-in window,
and the result of the first start (the EA app installed and registered, no account or machine state of EA's
anywhere). NOT tested: a second Mac, macOS 15, Gatekeeper and the quarantine steps above (they describe how
macOS behaves), EA's sign-in, the activation prompt, playing the game from this build, the microphone prompt,
or EA's own self-update. docs/NFS2015.md section 15 inside the app's source archive says exactly what was
checked and how.
